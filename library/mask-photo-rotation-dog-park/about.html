STORY

(The meaning behind the name)

Dogs and people.
Different in appearance, both are family.

But step just outside,
A line is drawn between ”dog” and ”person.”
A place we can’t be together, time we can’t spend together.
Families can end up split apart.

Even so,
They are not exactly the same.
Dogs have their own kind of comfort,
People have joys of their own.

For dogs. For people. But not only that.
I want to create a place where both can be enjoyed to the fullest.

A place where excited barking and laughter ring out together.
A relationship that accepts differences without drawing boundary lines.

A circle is a person’s face. A triangle is a dog’s face.
No shape is better than another.
When they overlap, new laughter is born.

That, together with this name
The heart we put into the logo.
