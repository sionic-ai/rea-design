Smartphone payment service

You can pay easily with your smartphone. Make daily grocery shopping convenient. Try a smartphone payment service linked to your bank account.

  • Pay OnePay OneSmartphone payment that earns and spends points at nearby shops. Pay and send money with a single app.
  • BrandBrandShop with just a smartphone. You can pay in stores.
  • AppCard PayPrepaid card that supports smartphone payment. It can also be used as a physical card.
  • Money TapMoney TapMoney transfer app that sends directly from a bank account with no top-up. Instantly, 24/7/365.
  • Mart PayMart PayPayment you can use right in your secondhand marketplace app. Use your sales proceeds right away, too.
  • Coin+Coin+A payment service for top-ups and transfers from a bank account. More places accept it every day.
  • SU-PAYShop PayA payment app you can use at supermarkets nationwide. Grocery points add up too.
