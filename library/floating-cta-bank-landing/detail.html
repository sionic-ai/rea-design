Goal-based accounts

Handy auto-save built in! Helps you save for each goal

Besides your main account, you can create up to 00 accounts named for a purpose. Split your savings for things like travel, a home or education, and move money in automatically on a set day each month.

+The difference between a main account and purpose-based accounts

Use scenes

  • Since the child was born

    Toward education funding
    Auto-save 00k every month

    Setting aside money needed for admission and school advancement in advance lets you save without spending it.

  • Because I wanted to travel

    Travel funds for the future
    Auto-save 0,000 won every day

    Saving little by little lets you build a travel fund without strain.

  • For managing living costs

    Sharing part of the pay
    Manage living expenses

    Splitting spending money from savings shows your monthly expenses at a glance.

Three features of purpose-based accounts

  • Feature 1

    Up to 00 items Name your account anything you like!

    Pick a name and icon to suit your purpose.

  • Feature 2

    Auto SavingsYou can do it!

    Set an amount and timing, and it is moved and saved automatically from the main account.

  • Feature 3

    KRW deposit + foreign currency depositcan be managed together!

    Foreign currency deposits go into purpose-based accounts too, so you can see everything in one place.

Account details by purpose

1. Create accounts by purpose

  1. STEP 1

    Tap ‹Create a goal-based account› above.

  2. STEP 2

    Enter a name for the account and press ”Next”.

  3. STEP 3

    Pick an icon and press ”OK” to finish.

2. Set an automatic savings rule for the account you created

  1. STEP 1

    Tap ”Auto-save setup”.

  2. STEP 2

    Set the amount and timing to transfer.

  3. STEP 3

    Check the details and press ‹Settings›.

How to deposit and withdraw from goal-based accounts

How to Pay

You can deposit by transferring from the main account, by auto-savings, or by transferring from another goal account.

Withdrawal Method

After moving funds to the main account, you can withdraw or transfer.

※ Direct ATM withdrawals and transfers cannot be made from purpose-based accounts.
