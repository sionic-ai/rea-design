OUR PHILOSOPHYMany professions connect,
Of people who work and the region
Supports the household.

This cooperative has work in many fields, including stores, delivery, dining, dispensing pharmacies and care.

Our starting point is to support the livelihoods of our working colleagues and their families. And to support people’s everyday lives in this region. That is our role.

Departments and duties differ, but we all head toward the same place: ‹Everyday Living›.

  • New grad hire

    Staying close to the community,
    Able to work with peace of mind

    Everyday work connects directly to contributing to the region. There are no transfers that require relocating.

  • High School Grad Entry-Level

    On many stages
    A widening career

    In broad businesses like stores, restaurants, delivery and care, you can deepen expertise in each job type while broadening your career.

  • Career

    Supporting the household
    A solid, reassuring stability.

    On top of a long foundation, it is an association supporting the basics of daily life, so it is an environment where you can work steadily for a long time.
