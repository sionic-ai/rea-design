ORIGINSThe natural peace of mind,
What is needed
For each and every person.

In 0000, during turbulent times of scarce supplies, this cooperative was born.
It began as a small ”mutual aid group” inside a factory.
It started from a sincere wish for people working together to support each other and secure the supplies needed for daily life.

Supporting the livelihoods of working people, like the shop and cafeteria, grew wider thanks to colleagues saying ”thank you.” In time we came to reach the livelihoods of people living in this neighborhood too.

Our basic principle is “the greatest service to our members.” It means making members’ lives better and better.

HISTORYThe Co-op’s Journey

From here, follow the steps along a timeline.

  1. 1945

    Started as a ”mutual aid group”

    Founded as a mutual-aid group within the factory

  2. 1949

    Establishing Organizational Structure

    Renamed to the current name and relaunched as a corporation

  3. 1976

    Milestone in business expansion

    Opens the new HQ store that will become the current main store

  4. 1985

    Expanding delivery services

    Started a group purchasing service for members who can’t make it to the store

  5. 1995

    Introducing a new name

    On its 50th anniversary, a friendly new nickname is chosen

  6. 2025

    80th anniversary of founding

    Set a new vision toward the next 100th anniversary
