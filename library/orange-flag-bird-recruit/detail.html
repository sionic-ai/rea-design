The local hero that supports daily livingGeneral Track

Through several businesses such as in-store dining support, parcel delivery, and restaurant operation, it supports the daily lives of the area and its members.

We listen closely to each person’s voice and plan and run services that lead to a safer, more comfortable household.

Recommended for general-track candidates

  • Someone who can turn a person’s ”smile” into their own strength
  • Someone who can think and work things out on their own and take on challenges
  • Someone who works sincerely and looks out for the people around the team
  • Someone who takes on broad challenges and can ”enjoy” them

MAIN JOB ROLESMain Duties

  1. 01Store businessSupermarkets and in-company shops

    Handles customer service, ordering and display of goods, and staff management at a supermarket or kiosk. Customer voices are used to shape the store.

  2. 02Parcel Delivery BusinessGroup buying · individual delivery

    We deliver ordered goods without fail. Beyond delivering goods, we also handle product proposals and route planning.

  3. 03Restaurant Service BusinessCompany cafeterias · hospital patient meals

    Handles cooking, meal planning, menu development and hygiene management at factories, offices, hospitals and more.

  4. 04Support DepartmentProduct sourcing, planning, management, etc.

    Merchandise purchasing, sales promotion planning, information systems, HR, general affairs, accounting and other functions support each business from behind the scenes.
