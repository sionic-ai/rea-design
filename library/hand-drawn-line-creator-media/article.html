The client digs out something to brag about naturally. | Studio name & company name [School 70th anniversary site]

── But the illustrations used here are matched one each to five departments, and each department has its own distinct color.

Name A ： Each illustration has great presence. So we felt its charm comes across better when they change one by one rather than all being shown at once.

If you only follow the plan, you won’t stray from the big direction. We felt that pairing words and images gives the whole a single grain.

── Name A also gave a talk in last year’s course, and said then, ”It is important to put into words why you make it.”

Name A / Studio Name

I think that in design, being able to put into words ‹why we do it this way› matters more than tidy organization. But finding that ‹why› is the hardest part.

■ Key point of the site ① ─ A scroll experience where ”the use of illustration changes”

Since this site organizes a vast amount of information, we took care to hold a lot of content without becoming monotonous. We revised it many times to preserve the reading rhythm while keeping the visuals pleasing.

■ The client creates ”room to think” and we choose together as we build, so it feels convincing

── What did you gain from this work?

Name B ： What I felt again is that to make a good result, it is important first to align views with the client. In branding and copy, the maker is not the only one holding the answer.
