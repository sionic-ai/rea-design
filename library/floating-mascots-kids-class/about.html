TOP · ABOUT BRAND

BRAND is a new learning space for children, supervised by a group of companies working in medical care, caregiving and preventive health. Building on the knowledge gained so far, doctors, physical therapists, dietitians and exercise instructors work together to support the growth of each child’s ”heart,” ”mind” and ”body.”

Reaching toward each person’s future and potential, we want to nurture the endlessly open tomorrow of a child — born from that wish.

With exercise, nutrition and sleep as pillars, parents and children grow a ”100-year moving body” through play. That is the kids wellness gym, BRAND.

”What can I do for this child?”
That one answer, BRAND It is.

Every one of us hopes that children will grow up strong and healthy.
But children today are already exposed to many health risks.

  • Decline in athletic ability since childhood
  • Effects on development from disrupted nutrition and sleep rhythms
  • Stiff bodies and rising injuries known as ”child locomo”

Early childhood in particular is a very important time for building basic lifestyle habits such as exercise, nutrition and sleep.
The blueprint of the body and mind is said to be almost fully drawn by around age six. Some studies even report that 80% of an adult’s brain nerves develop by age six. Moving your body through play leads to a body that moves and the power to think.
