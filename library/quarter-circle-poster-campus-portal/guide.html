Application Eligibility

A person who meets all of conditions (1) to (3) below.

  1. Anyone aged 00 or older as of April 1, 0000.
  2. A person with work experience.
  3. Applicants who meet one of the following and are eligible for university admission.
    (1) A person who has graduated from high school or secondary school
    (2) A person who has completed 12 years of school education through the regular course
    (3) A person recognized under the School Education Act enforcement rules as having academic ability equal to or higher than high school graduation
