Job support from the Career Support Center

Individual Consultation

We take counseling on employment as well as further study and career paths at any time. Even if you have not decided on a path or do not know where to start, feel free to reach out.

Résumé and entry sheet review

Corporate professionals review it. We polish your motivation and self-PR together to build documents that reach the reader. You can consult as many times as you like.

Mock interview

Practice in real formats from individual interviews to group interviews and group discussions. Review the recorded video together to find points to improve.

Career guidance

Guidance opens monthly from April of third year. It covers topics timed to the season such as self-analysis, industry research and interview prep.
