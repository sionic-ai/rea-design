Our promise about dental care

    Treat after resolving the root cause.(Emergency care is handled separately)

    Treatment begins only after safety is secured first.

    We provide the right time and environment so children can focus on treatment.(Treatments for preschool children or ones needing many staff are held on weekday mornings)

    Rubber dam isolation is the default for treatment, for safe and precise care.
