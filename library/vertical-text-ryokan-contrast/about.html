(Feature)

What this place stands for

What we are particular about is a space for enjoying bathing and cooking. Discover interiors designed for adult time and the things that color your journey.

All eight rooms have a full bay view, and the terrace has an open-air bath fed by fine hot spring water※. Interior, furniture, bedding, tableware and supplies were chosen with total care.

For meals, guests in the VIP wing get a private dining room next to their room, and main building guests get individual private dining rooms.

※ Only ”Lantern” has a semi-open-air bath

(Brand)

VIP hall

Besides the top-floor VIP room, the guest house has two special rooms. Each room takes a whole floor to itself.

Also, guests staying in the VIP room or the two special rooms can dine in a private dining room next to their room. You can choose table or floor seating, and there is no need to use the elevator.

Unlike eating in a regular room, a room set apart just for dining lets you enjoy a more focused time.

(Honkan)

Main building

The main building has rooms on floors 1 to 3, each with its own character. Guests on floors 2 and 3 dine in private dining rooms, and guests on floor 1 enjoy seasonal flavors in their rooms.
