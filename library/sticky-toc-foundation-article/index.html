「Project Name”: What Is the Project?

「Project Name」 is a project aiming for a future where everyone, with or without disabilities, can enjoy getting around and take on the new possibilities that mobility opens up. Ideas gathered from inside and outside are given shape, and with help from the people concerned we keep refining them as we move toward real-world adoption.

Six kinds of support toward realizing society

This project offers the six kinds of support below, tailored to the challenges and circumstances of each active team.
This support has continued thanks to people with many perspectives, including those directly affected, as well as welfare, business and technology experts. These encounters give the team new insights and push the work one step further. Also, guided by the idea of “on-site, firsthand,” we run field trials in real usage scenes and feed the vivid voices, issues and hints we gain into the next improvements.

0000 Activity Report

① ”Everyday Mobility” Real-world trials in the places and scenes where people live daily

For overseas teams, understanding the walking environment here and how visually impaired people walk is important. Insights gained from walking the park themselves or using products in daily life over a long time lead to products tailored to this place.
We also did field research on the street with the people directly affected, gaining vivid insights we could never get from a desk.

② “Watching a game” VENUE NAME Field trials at

We ran a field trial in the stands of a professional club’s home game at the stadium, with the goal that everyone, with or without visual or hearing impairments, can feel and enjoy the same moment together. Using several technologies such as AI, light and touch to convey the excitement of the match and the players’ movements, we confirmed the possibility of sharing anticipation and excitement together.
Also, the booth introducing this work was visited by many people who came to watch the game. It was a chance to meet a new viewing experience and understand ways of watching more deeply.

③Mixer

At the exchange meeting where the selected teams gather, we learned about interviewing the people concerned, designing field experiments, and thinking toward real-world implementation. Through workshop conversations and case sharing, the teams learn from each other and new connections are forming. During the time for exchanging opinions, we shared worries and ideas and gained hints that will lead to future activities.
