Q&AFrequently asked questions

  • Q

    Is it okay if I don’t know how to use professional software?

    A

    That’s fine. A program is just a tool for expression.
    We’ve prepared lectures and hands-on practice so you can learn from the basics after you join.
    Add one more line noting that those with basic skills can learn faster.

  • Q

    What kind of person fits this company?

    A

    Someone who loves thinking all the way through about whether there’s a better way to convey it.
    Someone with a sharp sense of trends and art who takes expression seriously.
    Add an answer that says: someone who tries first, unafraid of failure.

  • Q

    Is there anything worth doing while you’re a student?

    A

    Having experience creating works would be a great help.
    More important than that is an eye for what moves people’s hearts in expression.
    The experience of seriously facing what you love will surely come alive in your work too.

  • Q

    Can I take a break?

    A

    By default, weekends and public holidays are off, and there is a year-end holiday too.
    Add a note that a separate 5-consecutive-day leave program exists.
    Closes by recommending a short break away from work.

  • Q

    Are there rules for clothing or hairstyle?

    A

    There are no special rules for clothing or hairstyle.
    A note that neatness suited to the occasion of meeting still matters.
    Close by respecting the sense and posture that seep out from within, along with freedom of outward appearance.
