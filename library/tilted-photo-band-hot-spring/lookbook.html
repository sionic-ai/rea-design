About downloading photos

Two or three lines go here saying photos for promotion and guidance can be downloaded. Please inquire in advance about commercial use.

How to download (computer)

(1) Tap the one you want among the photos above.
(2) Press ”Download” under the enlarged photo.

Tablet · Smartphone

(1) Click the photo you want.
(2) Press and hold the photo to save it.

Notes

All copyright for the photos belongs to the lodging. Please do not edit or crop them. They may not be passed on or sold. We would appreciate it if you let us know after using them.
