INTRODUCTION

A lifelong strength for living,
Together with life in this town.

A small town alive with world heritage history and rich nature.
For children, the ordinary life that unfolds here every day is both play and learning.
Events that color real mountains, fields, old ruins and all four seasons.
And meet warm human connection, building a center for body and mind — a precious experience, right here.
Every one of them is a child of this town. Their smiles light up the town again today.

In a village of just over 400 residentsAdults and children are all friends.

In this small town, children find play all around the village. Adults they meet on the street call them by name, and the children greet them. The only thing the adults can do is ”watch over them.” The whole town is the playground.

Of the daycareFeature ①

From a life with earth and fireSavoring the joy of eating with your whole body.

Eating is living. Because we want you to feel how precious that is with your whole body, our center brings fieldwork and hearth cooking into daily life. We touch the soil, wait for the fruit, and share rice cooked over a wood fire together.
