The whole town is music, our town

Our town is shaped around the theme ”Music all over town.” Not bound by genre or form, we dream of a town where music can be heard at work, at school, in shops and on the street.

Centered on its pride, the concert hall, it enjoys music broadly: listening, singing and playing, as well as making instruments, painting pictures on music themes and writing stories.

Please come feel the free and generous music that overflows in our neighborhood.
