The hot spring
Origin story

The water of this hot spring
The memory of the land remains.
In this area, made up of hot spring minerals
”Travertine” can be seen in many places,
Since about 30,000 years ago
There are traces of water that once gushed.
Born of the grand work of nature, the hot spring,
In the flow of changing times,
It still keeps rising, unchanged.

Long, long ago

Your story of this era goes here, in about six lines. Long ago, corals that grew in the sea turned to stone and, after a long span of time, became part of this land. With volcanic heat and rainwater pooled underground, every condition came together and water began to rise. The milky color of the water comes from the minerals in that stone.

Era two

Story of the second era here. People who crossed the mountains began to seek this water to heal their bodies.

Era three

Your third era’s story goes here. Even amid war, the water of this valley kept springing up without rest.

Era four

Your fourth era story here. Inns welcoming hot-spring cure guests began to appear one after another.

Modern era

Your modern-era story here. Writers began visiting this hot spring and leaving words about it.

Era Six

Modern era

Your story of the modern era here. Roads were built and more people came to visit this valley.

Up to now

Your story of the present here. There are people who keep the old water while welcoming new guests.

Voice

Coming here seems to clear my senses more than usual and loosen my mind. You could say I can be honest about ‹what I feel›. When I get into the water, close my eyes and listen to the sounds of nature, I wonder if I was born here a very long time ago. There is a nostalgic scent.

Female in her 20s

Voice

When life gets busy, you get caught up in the feeling that you must keep moving forward. Still, now and then you long to stop. Here, where I found myself, soaking in white water amid the stillness of the mountains, I felt a strange sense of relief, as if I were being told ‹You don’t have to do anything.›

Men in their 40s
