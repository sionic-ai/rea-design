A limited edition controller commemorating the series’ 30th anniversary, ”Game Title Limited Edition,” is coming in limited quantities! Pre-orders open from October 22.

  • Posting
  • Share

The Game Title series turns 30 next year. To celebrate, a limited-edition controller with special graphics is coming in limited quantities.

We chose a race-car design: a white base with racing stripes and motorsport-inspired graphics, fitted with a front panel with a carbon pattern texture.

The series producer spoke about the thought put into this controller: “Thirty years ago, this series began with a wish to share the beauty of cars and the joy of driving. This commemorative controller carries the journey we have walked together with you. We sincerely thank each of you, whose passion still inspires us.”
