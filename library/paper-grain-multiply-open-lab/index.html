Brand、This could be the future

Here, whether you love art or love science,
Adults and kids alike,
A wide plaza where everyone can gather.

Here, a seed with no name yet
Fluffy soil, raised together by many.

A leisurely forest walk here.

Beside every small activity
The seed of the next dream is hiding here.

What shall we do here?
Where my new world begins to open.
A place where everyone’s wisdom is woven together.
Yes, maybe this is the future.

Welcome,
Person to person, curiosity to curiosity, all connected
To ”Commons for Everyone”

”Brand” is for more than Your School students and staff, but also
Neighbors,
An open space for inquiry for everyone, including children.

Continuing across generations and fields
Work that creates new exchanges and activities
Leads and helps.
Come on, let’s get excited together
Enjoy building the future.
