eye-follow — Move the mouse around the screen. The pupils follow the cursor (small eyes stay put since they have no room left). (After 1s one more comes in, and after 2.5s one drops out)

glance type — the pupils of the line-drawn face snap toward the cursor every 140ms

Spring type — move the mouse inside the panel below and the eyes follow along with a bounce (at the panel edge, 24% of their own size)
