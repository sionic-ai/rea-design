What we aim for

Our goal

Medical care that supports life, rooted in this region.

Your bold body text goes here. Having medical care by your side at any time. We felt this region needs a place that supports not only the body but the mind.

We want to reshape healthcare in this country from the ground up. Not a choice between going to the hospital or toughing it out at home, but being able to ”choose the care that fits you.”

To widen those choices, we combine home medical care, nursing and welfare into one, aiming for care that supports every stage of life.

Greeting

Greeting

Your greeting title here: From the field to the community, seamless care

Put the greeting body here. What we aim for is care that stays beside each patient’s life and takes responsibility to the end.

I used to work in emergency care. Facing patients whose lives were at risk, I felt how important medicine is, yet I was frustrated by encounters that lasted only that moment.

So after coming back, I put my effort into building the system that supports home-based care.

“Until the very last moment, being true to yourself.” We will keep building medical care that fulfills this wish, here in this region.

Your greeting title here: Everyday life lived with peace of mind, through the power of nursing

Put body text here. Passing through emergency care, I jumped into community medicine. I feel more strongly the value of people living with peace of mind as themselves.

From outpatient care to home visits, home nursing, rehab and facility care, we provide medical and nursing care that supports the community’s daily life and lives.

Keeping ”care that honors who the person is, staying by their side” in mind, we will help people smile and live well even with illness or disability.
