WORK FLOW

Work overview flow

STEP 01

Contact me on days you want to work (booking)

Once you’ve decided which days you’d like to work, please call the office.
Book the day you want.
※ We may also call to confirm whether you are available to work.

If there’s a day you want to come in, call us first!

STEP 02

Final check the day before

We call the day before your reservation to confirm your final decision.
Then explain the site location and the work involved, and once you agree, the job is formally settled the next day.
※If no job fits your preferred day, we may suggest a job on another day.

I’ll explain it properly, so don’t forget to call

STEP 03

Same-day contact (departure·arrival)

On the day, please call us once when leaving home and again on arriving at the meeting place.

I just want to check you’re getting there okay~

STEP 04

Start work on site

Work follows the site leader’s instructions.

For first-timers, we ask about the day’s attire in advance and pass it on to the leader.
On the day, the leader will speak to you first, so don’t worry.

I won’t just drop it all of a sudden!

STEP 05

Work ends → pay delivered

When the job is done, we hand you your pay in cash at the office that same day.
If it’s hard to receive it on the day, you may receive it at your convenience.

I’ll give it to you that day! Come whenever you can!

STEP 06

Short recap

Hand over the pay and casually ask, ”How was work today?”
We listen closely so the next job fits even better.

If any site doesn’t fit, please tell us!
