Message

Greeting

”Opening your eyes” is the root of our clinic’s logo. It carries our hope to keep your vision clear and help you find new possibilities and joy, and our resolve to raise the value of eye care.

The eye is a small organ about 2cm across, yet a window that reflects the great joys of life. We want to protect that 「small window」 and support your every day.

In addition to specialized medical care, we value being easy to ask and easy to talk to, and will build an environment where you can consult comfortably. We will also be a presence that supports the community through eye health.

Feel free to consult us about any eye concern. From professional treatment to everyday care, we will answer with sincerity.
