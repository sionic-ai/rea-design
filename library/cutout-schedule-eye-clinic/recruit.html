Personality

Who We’re Looking For

  • I can take the joy of patients and colleagues as my own.

    It is enough to greet people first with a smile. We value a heart that feels the joy of patients and colleagues as your own.

  • ”Something New”
    A person who accepts things with joy

    You don’t have to be good at everything from the start. We value a forward-looking mind that can be curious about new technology and services and think ”sounds fun.”

  • “What I can do”
    Someone who wants to widen their world little by little.

    Your “I want to try” spirit leads to a better clinic. We wholeheartedly support those who set goals and work toward them, and those who want to take on new challenges.

  • Keeping time and promises
    Person who moves swiftly

    Doing the obvious builds trust. If you work briskly, or are comfortable with computers and phones, you can put that strength to use. Let’s take it one step at a time together.

  • People growing together

    Healthcare is a team effort. We value colleagues who recognize each other’s efforts and lend a hand. When things get tough, a senior colleague stands right beside you, so you can work with peace of mind.

Culture

”Glad we chose this place”
Building a workplace people are proud of.

We want to build together an environment where all staff can work with peace of mind and keep looking ahead.
We value ease of work and a sense of fulfillment, and want to grow a clinic trusted by the community together.
We will do our best so you can say “I’m glad I work here.”

    A new start for everyone
    To a place where you can

    As a new clinic opening in 2026, we build our systems and culture together with the staff. The whole team backs you up so you can settle in quickly even if you join midway.

    For those with no experience or on a break
    Division Welcome

    We offer step-by-step training and support tailored to each person, whatever your experience or age. We aim for an open, easygoing workplace, so feel free to ask about anything you don’t know.

  3. 03

    Flexible working,
    We’re cheering you on.

    We’ll accommodate work that fits around housework and daily life as far as we can. Let’s build an environment together where you can work comfortably for a long time.
