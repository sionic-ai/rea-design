Experience workshop (short)

Updated 00/0000

All sessions of this year’s experience workshops are over.

The experience workshop is a chance to try the participatory classes that form the foundation of learning at this School.
Taking part can prevent career choices from going wrong. Be sure to check whether learning in person suits you.
Also, taking part in the workshop makes you eligible to apply for this track. To apply, you must take part.
Applicants under the recommendation track must also take part in either a workshop or an experience event.
