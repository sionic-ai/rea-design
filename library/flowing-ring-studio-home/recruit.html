Open positions

Producer

We take charge of the entire project, set goals together with the client and lead the team from planning to execution. We make judgments that connect business and expression.

Application guidelines

People searching

For us, who stand beside clients to contribute to their business, sincerity and responsibility matter. On top of that, we look for someone passionate about making and thinking, who can enjoy even uncertain situations and lead projects.

Technology that welcomes

  • Experience working at a digital production or creative agency
  • Experience working as a producer, director, or project manager

The appeal of the work and what’s ahead

The biggest appeal of working here is being involved from the very start of a project through to the finished result. The work ranges from building real-world spaces to web production and research-like art projects, so you can develop core planning and production skills that carry over to any industry. For anyone who wants to go independent someday, or bring new value to the market with their own hands, this is a place to test what is possible.
