Philosophy

Blending together, shaping form.

”I want to make it” ”I want to improve it” ”I want to see it”

Not only strong will, but a small realization or something that has no shape yet.
Every heart holds the same possibility.
What matters is how good a shape you can give that heart.
To do that, standing beside, digging in, and finding out
A good process, a good team, and the right environment are essential.

Just as abundant fruit needs abundant soil,
Accepting every wish, it shapes a form that lives and breathes.
Earnest in making, and flexible too.
We pursue a better shape for that heart.
