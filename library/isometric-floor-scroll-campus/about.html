About Our CampusWhat a campus is

Your introduction here of the building with two basement floors and eight above ground. A base for putting many attempts at collaborating with society into practice, with floors offering varied functions such as a co-creation space and a coworking space in sequence.

A new hub for industry-academia collaboration that encourages ties with companies, local governments and community groups, aiming for a campus open to society.

Your campus philosophy statement here. As a lab for innovation, it nurtures talent that pioneers the future and is a place of practice that proposes new value to society.

Features of Our CampusCampus features

The ground floor is a café and workshop space that is also open to the public. Staff, students and local residents mix naturally and new conversations begin. With small shops and an event space, experiments worthy of an education and research institution continue.

Floors 5 through 7 hold labs, classrooms, and joint research hubs. Shared by graduate programs and working-adult programs, these floors host collaboration across departments and fields.

The basement has a high-ceilinged multipurpose project space and a studio. It is made for large-scale work such as exhibitions, installations and video shoots.
