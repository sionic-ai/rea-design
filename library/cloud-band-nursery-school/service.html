Commitment to Dining

Make, taste, and give thanks.
Through meat Healthy mindNurtures

Early childhood eating habits last a lifetime
The foundation for building a body that lives healthily.
Our school nurtures children’s healthy minds and bodies,
Cherishes eating, the foundation of it all.

1Cherishing the goodness of traditional food

Preparing meals starts each morning with making the broth. To prevent lifestyle diseases that develop in adulthood, it matters to get used to mild flavors from infancy.

2Taste the season

We aim to put seasonal ingredients on the menu where possible, so children feel the seasons and grow rich sensibilities. That is why we value eating locally what is grown locally.

3Cherish the act of chewing

Building chewing strength has many benefits such as ”better nutrient absorption,” ”preventing obesity,” ”preventing cavities,” and ”supporting brain development.”

4Garden Plot Activity

Growing their own food, children naturally take an interest in crops and meals. Watching them grow, a heart of gratitude for receiving that life grows too.

5Cooking Childcare

Cooking uses all five senses: seeing, smelling, hearing, touching and tasting. Cooking together deepens interest in food.

6Allergy · Baby food

Substitute meals are served on name-tagged trays and color-coded tableware so they never go to the wrong person.
Baby food is prepared separately for each child to suit their development, in contact with the family.

An automated external defibrillator is available

An automated external defibrillator is kept on hand just in case.

Crime and Accident Prevention

In preparation for suspicious persons, security cameras are placed throughout the school and a contract is made with a security company so they rush over right away in case of intrusion.
Also, every nursery room has an indoor camera so you can watch over everything from another room.
