About the Hospital

About clinic

Building a mouth that’s easy to protect,
A dental clinic you can visit for life.

The MI in our name comes from a phrase meaning a minimal touch.
Based on the idea of ”cutting teeth as little as possible”
We value ”care that protects your teeth for life.”

What we hope for is that patients keep healthy teeth for life.
For that, prevention matters, not only treatment.
We want to make mouths easy to protect from problems, and be a clinic people can visit for many years.

Prevention doesn’t end at the hospital alone.
As a companion walking alongside patients, we will support their daily care.

POLICYOur clinic’s three principles

Checking every corner,
Thorough exams and diagnosis

We use a microscope that lets you see inside the mouth at about 24 times the naked eye.
You can check the cause of the problem more thoroughly
Leads to treatment that avoids cutting away as much as possible.

In a separate room prepared for you,
Calm consultation

There are also consultation rooms, so you needn’t mind other people
You can tell us what you wonder about and what you hope for.
We talk thoroughly before treatment.

Tailored to my taste,
Custom prevention

Not only how the teeth are lined up but also their shape
Looking even at lifestyle habits to tailor care to each person
We decide on prevention together.
