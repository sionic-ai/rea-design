The peach doll
Let’s make it

Thinking

Look at the design image and size, and think about how to show the fabric and face parts (embroidery, plastic parts, felt, etc.)

Pattern making and cutting

Make a pattern from paper like a paper craft, then add seam allowance to the unfolded pattern and cut the fabric to match.

Stitching

Sewn by sewing machine. Parts are small and curvy, so instead of pins, hold them with tweezers or an awl to stitch without slipping.

Stuffing

Even after stuffing, check the balance and adjust the pattern again and again, getting closer to completion. Finally the opening used for stuffing is closed with a whipstitch.

Finished

Attach the parts and it’s done. The leaves were made as separate parts and sewn onto the body with thread.
