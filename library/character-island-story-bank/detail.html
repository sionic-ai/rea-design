Check the identity verification steps in the app

Describe in one line the flow of choosing a bank in the app and picking an identity verification method.

※ Note that screen names may differ.

  1. STEP 1

    Choose an account registration menu

    On the app home screen, tap ”Top up” → ”Bank account” → ”Register account” in order.

  2. STEP 2

    Choose a financial institution

    Choose your bank here from the list that appears.

  3. STEP 3

    Choose identity verification method

    Select “Verify identity at the ATM”. Check the guidance on the next screen and press [Next].

  4. STEP 4

    Get verification code

    Press ”Get confirmation number” and the number to use at the ATM appears on screen. Please save the screen.

  5. STEP 5

    To the nearest ATM

    Take your number and head to the nearest ATM.
