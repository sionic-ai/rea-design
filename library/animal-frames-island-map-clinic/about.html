As the bridge of community healthcare.

To the doctors at local clinics and hospitals
Patients referred by others are also gladly welcome.

For example, only light therapy is done here and
Next, just as if continuing on from the original medical institution
Flexible coordination tailored to the situation is possible.

Also tailored to referrals to a university hospital or regional core hospital when needed
We value a gentle, strain-free flow of treatment.

We have opened a window to care.
A letter of introduction is not required.

Doctors and clinics, there is no need to write a referral letter — just tell patients ”Please go here.” If you do write a referral, noting details such as ”symptoms to be treated after leaving your hospital” lets us guide them smoothly.

(Director’s greeting)

Opened in this area in 2010, we have been here for over fifteen years.

We started as a small clinic with three nurses, four office staff and me as the only doctor. Today we have eleven nurses, fifteen office staff and fifteen doctors including part-timers, along with equipment such as light therapy.

In 2024 we also opened a new building for hair loss and cosmetic treatments. Patient card numbers have passed the 00000 range, and so far more than 000k people have visited us.

I was born in this region and grew up feeling its scenery and the warmth of its people close by. The wish to create a place where people living here can go with peace of mind has not changed from the day we opened until now.

Thanks to the help of many people the clinic has grown a lot, but we want to keep cherishing our starting point of listening to each person’s story and thinking from the patient’s seat.

We hope to remain a local clinic that people trust, where they feel ”it’s okay to come here” for years to come.
