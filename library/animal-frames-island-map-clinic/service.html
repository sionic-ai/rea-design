( About Name )

What is a condition name?

What comes from outward looks
A skin condition where emotional stress is also easily felt

Many people worry, ”Will this redness never go away?” This condition is a long-lasting inflammatory skin disease in which the skin turns red and swollen, and silvery-white flakes like dandruff form on top and fall off in showers.

The number of patients is rising every year, and changes in lifestyle habits are thought to be behind it. It is not contagious, but because of its appearance it easily causes mental stress and greatly lowers quality of life.

If left untreated, skin symptoms can spread across the whole body and may also cause joint pain or deformity. Recently, links with systemic conditions such as metabolic syndrome and heart disease have also been reported.

Not just the skin,
A condition linked to diseases throughout the body

This condition is understood not as a skin-only disease but as chronic inflammation of the whole body. When inflammation persists in the body, arteries harden more easily, and there are reports that the risk of vascular diseases such as heart attack and stroke rises, especially in moderate or severe cases.

What matters is not leaving this condition untreated. Treating it properly under a dermatologist and calming inflammation inside the body, along with caring for lifestyle habits such as blood pressure, blood sugar, lipids and smoking, is thought to lower risk throughout the body.

Don’t decide alone that ”it’s only mild.” Consulting a specialist early is the first step toward protecting your future health.

( Causes )

Causes of the condition

There is more than one cause

Many patients also wonder, ”Why me?” and ”Did I inherit this from my parents?”

Even modern medicine has not fully clarified the cause, but the main cause is thought to be an “immune abnormality.” It is believed to arise when various external and internal factors overlap in people with a susceptible constitution.

Specific triggers include excessive stress, irregular lifestyle, lack of sleep, obesity, smoking, infections, and the use of certain medications.

( Symptoms )

Symptoms of the condition

Worries like ‹When I take off my clothes, flakes fall off and I can’t wear black› and ‹The itching keeps me awake at night› are often heard.

( Typical symptoms )

  • One symptom name (How to read)

    A symptom where the skin swells up red with sharply defined edges.

  • Symptom name two (How to read)

    The red area swells thickly and feels slightly firmer to the touch than elsewhere.

  • Symptom name three (How to read)

    Appearing over the red spot
    Thick silvery-white scales layered up like dandruff.

These tend to appear on areas that easily get friction or irritation from outside, such as the scalp, outer elbows, knees and hips. In some people, small pits form in the nails, or the nails become deformed or thickened.

( Treatment )

Treatment of the condition

Matched to the weight of symptoms and your daily life,
Four treatments are used together

”I keep using the same ointment but it barely improves” — if your treatment is stuck, be sure to learn about new treatment options.

Treatment mainly falls into four types: ”topical medicine”, ”oral medicine”, ”phototherapy”, and ”biologics (injections)”. These are combined to fit symptom severity and the patient’s lifestyle as treatment proceeds.

Recently, oral medication options have also widened, and new options have appeared in addition to the earlier oral drugs. For severe cases, biologic agents can also be expected to work very well, so we propose a treatment suited to each patient as needed.

( Features and strengths )

Features and Strengths

Supporting the treatment of disease,
Well-equipped light therapy facilities

Here we put special focus on phototherapy. Phototherapy is a treatment that picks out only the specific wavelengths of the sun’s ultraviolet light with high therapeutic effect and few side effects, and shines them on the skin to suppress an overactive immune response.

The biggest strength here is matching several of the latest light therapy devices to symptoms and areas (Device One, Device Two, Device Three) can be shared out, and that’s the key point.
