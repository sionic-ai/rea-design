## About the rebuilt sites

Each library site is an original rebuild, written from scratch to study the layout, proportions and motion of a well-made public website. No text, images, logos, video or source code from those sites is included: copy is English placeholder text, pictures and videos are generated placeholders, and drawings that stood in for artwork are simple shapes. Site names and domains have been removed and every site carries a neutral descriptive slug.

Use the library as a reference for structure and motion, and bring your own content and visual identity. If you believe a page still resembles a specific site too closely or carries an identifying trace, please open an issue and we will fix or remove it.
